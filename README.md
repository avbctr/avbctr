### Hi, I'm Anderson ∴

I'm a **Software Architect & AI Engineer** with nearly 20 years in the **.NET** ecosystem. I turn complex enterprise systems into **scalable, secure, AI-ready platforms**: microservices, ERP and legacy integrations, and AI-native capabilities exposed to agents through **MCP (Model Context Protocol)**, with clear standards for architecture, observability and API governance.

Right now I work on two fronts. At **GEP** I lead the technical evolution of an AI-native, multi-agent SaaS platform, including the move to **.NET 10**, a new authentication model with **rotating refresh tokens** and **parallel sub-agents with per-phase telemetry**. At **CH Master Data** I lead the customization squad and own **CI/CD and code governance** for the development teams.

I work decision first: diagnose before changing anything, record decisions in ADRs and RFCs, turn standards into build and pipeline rules, and ship small, traceable changes, using AI coding agents under my review. Self-taught, technical author, and driven by reliability and engineering craft.
